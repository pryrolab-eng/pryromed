---
title: Refunds & Returns
description: Processing refunds and returns in Pryrox POS
---

Refunds must always reference the original sale receipt number. The system issues an **NR (Normal Refund)** receipt with negative amounts, linked to the original NS receipt.

## Steps

1. Click **Returns / Refund** in POS
2. Enter the original receipt number or scan its QR code
3. Select items, quantity, reason, and disposition
4. Click **Process Return**

## Dispositions

| Disposition | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| **Restock** | Items go back into inventory |
| **Damaged** | Items removed from stock, logged as damaged |
| **Destroy** | Items destroyed, removed from stock |

## Rules

- Each original receipt can only be cancelled once
- Refund amounts are shown with a negative (−) sign on the receipt
- The NR receipt includes `REF. NORMAL RECEIPT#: [original receipt number]`
