---
title: Cashier Shifts
description: Opening, managing, and closing cashier shifts
---

Pryrox requires an open shift before processing any sale or return. Shifts track the cashier's session, cash float, sales totals, and trigger the Z-Report on close.

## Opening a shift

1. Go to POS → **Shift** panel
2. Enter the opening cash amount
3. Click **Open Shift**

## During a shift

The shift panel shows live totals:
- Total sales
- Total refunds
- Transaction count
- Time open

## Closing a shift

1. Click **Close Shift**
2. Count the physical cash and enter the actual amount
3. Click **Confirm Close**

On close, the system automatically submits the **Z-Report** to the VSDC. See [Z-Report](/ebm/z-report) for details.

## Training mode

Training mode can be toggled **separately** from the normal sale flow. See [Training Mode](/guides/training-mode).
