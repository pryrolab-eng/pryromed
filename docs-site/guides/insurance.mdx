## How it works

1. Select **Insurance** as the payment method (or use Split)
2. Choose the insurance provider (RSSB, MMI, etc.)
3. Enter the patient's membership/beneficiary number
4. Pryrox calculates coverage and patient copay automatically
5. Both amounts appear as separate lines on the fiscal receipt

## Receipt layout (insurance)

```
Amoxicillin 250mg x2    3,000 RWF
─────────────────────────
TOTAL                   3,000 RWF
TOTAL TAX B               457.63 RWF
─────────────────────────
Insurance (RSSB 80%)   −2,400 RWF
Patient pays              600 RWF
```

## VSDC payload

For insurance sales, Pryrox includes **two separate item entries** in the VSDC payload — one for the insurer's share and one for the patient copay — so the fiscal receipt correctly itemises both.

## Claims

Insurance claims are created automatically and available in **Reports → Insurance Claims**.
