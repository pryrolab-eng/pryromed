## Overview

This manual covers daily POS operations for cashiers and pharmacy managers — from opening a shift to processing sales, refunds, and Z-reports.

<Tip>
  New to Pryrox? [Try the demo](/demo) to follow along with real sample data.
</Tip>

## Opening a cashier shift

You must open a shift before processing any sale. The system blocks transactions if no shift is open.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Shift panel">
    In the POS screen, click the **Shift** panel button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter opening cash">
    Enter the cash amount currently in the till.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Open Shift**. The system records your name and the opening time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Processing a Normal Sale (NS)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for the product">
    Type the medication name, scan the barcode, or filter by category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to cart">
    Click the product to add it. Adjust quantity if needed. The system automatically selects the earliest-expiry batch (FEFO).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle warnings">
    - **Near-expiry warning**: acknowledge to continue selling
    - **Prescription required**: confirm prescriber name and patient name
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select payment method">
    Choose Cash, Card, Mobile Money, Insurance, or Split payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the sale">
    Click **Complete Sale**. The sale is submitted to the VSDC and a signed fiscal receipt is generated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If the VSDC does not respond within 5 seconds, the sale is queued automatically. The receipt will show **"EBM pending"** until the VSDC reconnects.
</Warning>

## Fiscal receipt fields

Every normal sale receipt includes an **SDC Information block** required by RRA:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **SDC ID** | VSDC serial number assigned to your pharmacy |
| **MRC** | Machine Registration Code |
| **Receipt Number** | Sequential counter, e.g. `168/258 NS` |
| **Date/Time** | Authoritative VSDC timestamp |
| **Internal Data** | Encrypted RRA verification data |
| **Receipt Signature** | Cryptographic proof of authenticity |
| **QR Code** | Encodes date, time, SDC ID, receipt number, internal data, signature |

## Processing a Refund (NR)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Returns">
    Click **Returns / Refund** in the POS screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the original sale">
    Enter the receipt number or scan the QR code from the original receipt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select items and reason">
    Choose items to return, quantity, reason, and disposition (restock / damaged / destroy).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process">
    Click **Process Return**. An NR receipt is issued with negative amounts, linked to the original NS receipt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Training Mode

<Warning>
  Training mode must be activated using a **dedicated button** — it cannot be entered accidentally during a normal sale (RRA requirement).
</Warning>

1. Click the **Training Mode** toggle (separate from normal sale buttons)
2. A prominent **TRAINING MODE** banner appears
3. All receipts are TS/TR type — NOT submitted to the VSDC
4. Click the toggle again to return to normal sales mode

## Closing a shift

1. Open the **Shift** panel
2. Click **Close Shift**
3. Enter the actual cash in the till
4. Click **Confirm Close**

The system automatically submits the **Z-Report** to the VSDC when you close the shift.
