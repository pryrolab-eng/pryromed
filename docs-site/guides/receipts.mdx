Every completed sale generates a fiscal receipt containing the **SDC Information block** required by RRA.

## Receipt anatomy

```
Trade Name
Address Line 1
Address Line 2
TIN: 999000099
─────────────────────────
Amoxicillin 250mg x2    3,000 RWF  B
─────────────────────────
TOTAL                   3,000 RWF
TOTAL B-18.00%          3,000 RWF
TOTAL TAX B               457.63 RWF
─────────────────────────
CASH                    3,000 RWF
ITEMS NUMBER: 1
─────────────────────────
SDC INFORMATION
Date: 08/05/2026  Time: 14:23:11
SDC ID: SDC001000001
RECEIPT NUMBER: 168/258 NS
Internal Data: TE68-SLA2-34J5-EAV3
Signature: V249-J39C-FJ48-HE2W
[QR CODE]
─────────────────────────
RECEIPT NUMBER: 152
DATE: 08/05/2026  TIME: 14:25:00
MRC: AAACC123456
```

## Print formats

| Format | Use case |
|--------|---------|
| `roll` | Thermal 80mm printer (default) |
| `a5` | A5 paper |
| `a4` | A4 paper / full invoice |

## Reprint as COPY

To reprint a receipt, find it in **Sales History** and click **Print Copy**. The reprint is issued as **CS** (Copy Sale) or **CR** (Copy Refund) and clearly marked `COPY` and `THIS IS NOT AN OFFICIAL RECEIPT`.
