## What is Pryrox?

Pryrox is a cloud-based pharmacy management platform built for Rwanda. It handles everything a pharmacy needs — from point-of-sale to inventory, insurance billing, and full compliance with the **Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA) Electronic Billing Machine (EBM)** requirements.

## Key features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Point of Sale (POS)">
    Fast, barcode-enabled sales screen with FEFO batch selection, near-expiry warnings, prescription gating, multi-payment support (cash, card, mobile money, insurance, split), and cashier shift management.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="RRA EBM Compliance">
    Full VSDC API integration — NS/NR/CS/CR/TS/TR/PS receipt types, receipt signing, QR codes, Z-reports, stock synchronisation, and offline queue with automatic retry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Inventory Management">
    Multi-branch stock control, batch tracking, expiry alerts, purchase orders, stock transfers, and low-stock notifications.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Insurance Billing">
    Integrated insurance provider management, formulary coverage, copay calculation, and insurance claim generation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Multi-branch Support">
    Each branch operates independently with its own stock and receipts, while the head office has consolidated visibility and reporting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
